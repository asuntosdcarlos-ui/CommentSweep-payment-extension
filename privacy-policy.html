CommentSweep

Privacy Policy — Last updated: November 2026

1. Overview

CommentSweep ("we", "our", or "the extension") is a browser extension that helps YouTube creators find and delete unwanted comments from their own channel using keyword filters. It is operated by Carlos Martínez Polanco, an independent developer based in the Dominican Republic. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about the data we use.

2. YouTube API Services

CommentSweep uses YouTube API Services. By using CommentSweep you are also agreeing to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.

Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

You can revoke CommentSweep's access to your YouTube data at any time through the Google security settings page.

3. What we access through the YouTube Data API

After you sign in with Google, CommentSweep uses the YouTube Data API v3 to act on your behalf, only on the channel of the signed-in account:

  • Your channel information — to identify your channel and use its ID as your account identifier.
  • Your video list — to know which videos can be scanned.
  • Comments on your videos — to find and display the comments matching the keywords you typed.
  • Comment deletion — only for the comments you explicitly select and confirm.

The permission we request is youtube.force-ssl, which is the scope YouTube requires in order to delete or moderate comments. CommentSweep does not upload videos, change your video settings, post comments, or modify your channel in any other way.

4. Data we store

We store the minimum data necessary to run the service:

  • Your YouTube channel ID — stored in Firebase Firestore (Google Cloud) and used only to verify whether your subscription is active.
  • Your subscription status — whether the subscription is active or cancelled, and the date access expires. It is written only by our payment webhook, when PayPal notifies us of a change.

That is all we store on our servers. The following stays on your own computer:

  • Your Google access token — encrypted with AES-256-GCM and kept in your browser's local storage. It is never sent to our servers and it expires after about one hour.
  • Your language preference and keywords — kept in your browser so the extension remembers them.
  • Scan results — kept temporarily in your browser so you can review them, and cleared after use.

5. Data we do NOT collect

  • We do not store the content of your comments on any server. Comments are processed in your browser.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, password, or any other personal information through the extension itself.
  • We do not read, store, or process the content of your videos.
  • We do not track your browsing history or any activity outside the extension.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell or share it with third parties.
  • We do not use your YouTube data to train machine learning or AI models.

6. Third-party services

  • Google / YouTube Data API v3 — authentication and access to the comments on your channel. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Firestore (Google) — stores your channel ID and subscription status. See the Firebase Privacy Policy.
  • PayPal — processes subscription payments. We never receive or store card or bank details. See the PayPal Privacy Policy.
  • EmailJS — delivers your feedback message to us, and only when you choose to send one. The email address and message you type in that form reach us by email and are not kept in a database. See the EmailJS Privacy Policy.

7. How long we keep data

  • Your subscription record is kept while your subscription is active, and for up to 12 months afterwards as a billing and dispute record. You may ask us to delete it sooner.
  • Your access token expires after about one hour and is deleted from your browser when you sign out.
  • Scan results are cleared from your browser after use, and in any case when you leave the results screen.
  • Data obtained from the YouTube API is not kept beyond the browser session in which it is used.

8. Your rights and how to delete your data

You can do all of the following at any time:

  • Revoke our access to YouTube — through the Google security settings page. This takes effect immediately.
  • Clear the local data — sign out in the extension, or uninstall it from your browser.
  • Cancel your subscription — in your PayPal account, under Automatic payments.
  • Request deletion of your stored data — write to asuntosdcarlos@gmail.com from the email address associated with your PayPal subscription, or tell us your channel ID. We will delete your subscription record and confirm by email, normally within 30 days.
  • Ask what we hold about you — write to the same address and we will tell you.

Please cancel your subscription in PayPal before asking us to delete your record. If the subscription is still active, a later payment notification from PayPal would create the record again.

9. Security

Your Google access token is encrypted with AES-256-GCM before being stored in your browser. All communication with external services uses HTTPS. Our subscription database can only be written by our server-side payment webhook, and a record can only be read by the channel ID it belongs to. We never have access to your Google password or to your payment details.

10. Children's privacy

CommentSweep is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. International users

Our subscription data is stored on Google Cloud servers in the United States. If you use CommentSweep from another country, you understand that this limited data — your channel ID and subscription status — is processed there.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the extension after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

13. Contact

For any question about this policy, or to request access to or deletion of your data, contact us at asuntosdcarlos@gmail.com.
